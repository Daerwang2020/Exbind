# Third-party materials and exclusions

This release contains only project-generated benchmark records and software covered by the scoped license files.

The paper also reports an inventory of inherited SVG/DOM resources. Their raw records, screenshots, markup, and derived files are excluded because redistribution rights were not verified in the source project. Specifically excluded are EVG-Bench, EVG-Bench-Perturbed, Novel-SVG, SEB, SVGEditBenchV2, MiniWoB/DOM transfer assets, model weights, and cached model outputs. Users must obtain those resources from their upstream sources and follow the upstream terms.

No model weights, API keys, endpoints, inference clients, private logs, or author-specific paths are included.
